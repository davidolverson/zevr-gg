Underneath

Two systems. Neither decides.

The Talent Network makes you visible. The Grind remembers what you did. Neither is a ranking.

The Talent Network

An open pool, opened by a real opportunity and read by the people staffing it. Not a queue that expires.

The Grind

Evidence with its context kept. A scrim, a qualifier and a final are not the same thing.

Explainable, to you

What ZEVR holds about you can be shown to you: what it is, and where it came from.

The Grind is not a score. No single number ranks people against each other. There will not be one.

No AI decides who joins, plays, or leaves. Systems may organise evidence. Named people decide, and they record why.

A competitive program uses this path. A creator or a personality does not.
