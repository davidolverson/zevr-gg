Privacy

Website privacy notice.

This notice covers the public ZEVR website at zevr.gg: what it collects, what it does not, and what happens when you contact ZEVR through it.

Last updated 14 September 2026.

Scope

What this covers.

This notice applies to the zevr.gg website and nothing else.

The site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no member area, no applications and no forms. There is nothing on it to submit and no service behind it to submit anything to.

ZEVR is building member, creator and competition systems. None of them are live, none of them are reachable from this site, and when they exist they will have their own notice describing what they actually do. This page is not that notice and does not speak for them.

What the site collects

Nothing.

Not as a position, as a description. There is no mechanism on this site that collects anything about you.

  • No cookies. The site sets none, for any purpose, including functional ones.
  • No analytics or tracking. No analytics product, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no session recording, no fingerprinting.
  • No third-party requests. Your browser is not sent to any other company to load this site. Fonts are served from zevr.gg rather than a font CDN, and there are no embedded players, maps, widgets or social embeds.
  • No browser storage. Nothing is written to localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB.
  • No forms, uploads or email capture. There is nowhere on this site to enter your name, your email address, or anything else.

There is nothing here to request, correct or delete. A privacy request usually means asking an organization for the record it holds about you. This website does not create one. If that changes — because a form, an account or a mailing list appears — this notice changes with it, before the thing ships.

Hosting

What the host sees.

The one thing about a visit that is recorded anywhere is recorded by the infrastructure serving the files, not by ZEVR.

zevr.gg is served as static files from GitHub Pages. Like any web server, the hosting infrastructure processes standard request information — such as your IP address, the page requested, and your browser's user-agent string — in order to deliver the page and to operate and protect the service. That handling is GitHub's, under its own privacy statement.

ZEVR does not receive per-visitor logs from that infrastructure, does not have an analytics view built on it, and does not combine it with anything else.

Contacting ZEVR

What happens when you reach out.

You can email ZEVR, or reach it on public channels operated by other companies under their own terms.

If you email business@zevr.gg, your message and your email address reach the people operating ZEVR and are kept in that inbox so the conversation can continue. The site itself never sees or stores it: the email link only opens your own mail app.

If you message ZEVR on Discord, X, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or Twitch, that conversation lives on that platform, is handled under that platform's privacy practices, and is visible to the people operating ZEVR's presence there. Share only what you are comfortable sharing in that setting — in particular, do not send documents, identification, or anything sensitive.

Anything you choose to tell ZEVR in such a conversation is used to answer you and to have the conversation you started. It is not added to a marketing list, because there is not one.

The public channel is discord.gg/zevr.

Links out

Where this site sends you.

Outbound links go to Discord, X, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and Twitch, and nowhere else. The one other link opens your own mail app addressed to business@zevr.gg.

Once you follow one, you are on their site under their rules. This notice stops at the edge of zevr.gg.

If this notice and the site ever disagree, the site is the thing that is wrong. Raise it through the public Discord and it gets fixed.
