Creators

Made by people who craft.

ZEVR works with creators and personalities, and it makes things of its own. This page is about both: what working with ZEVR is meant to do for a creator, and the craft roles that make ZEVR's own work good.

Nothing is open yet — no creator programs, no openings, no submission inbox. What follows is how it is built to work, said before it is offered rather than after.

For creators

What ZEVR is for, if you make things.

Reach is the easiest thing in this industry to buy and the hardest to keep. The useful version is a company whose other work makes your work land further.

One moment, everywhere

A moment worth watching becomes the short edit, the social cut, the longer piece and the campaign. Most creators do all of that alone, badly, at 2am. That is the part a media operation is for.

A bigger stage than you have alone

ZEVR's competition, media and partnership work is where a creator reaches rooms they would not get into on their own. That is the reason these sit inside one company instead of six.

Partnerships that fit

Brand work matched to what someone actually makes, rather than whoever was cheapest and available. A bad fit costs the creator their audience's trust, and they are the one who pays for it.

There is no follower requirement. Not a minimum, not a preference, not a tiebreaker. An audience is something you may have; it is not evidence that you are good, and making it the entry condition is how organizations end up with well-followed work nobody wants to watch.

Credit and ownership

Your name stays on it.

How contribution is recorded is the part most creator arrangements leave vague, so it is stated here first rather than raised later.

Attributed

Work is credited to the person who made it, in the organization's record, and that attribution survives their departure.

Not taken by default

ZEVR does not assume ownership of everything someone makes because they work with ZEVR. What the organization commissions and what you make on your own are different things, and the difference is written down before work starts.

Portable

A record of what you built here is yours to take. That is the point of keeping one.

Reach is not offered instead of payment. Where ZEVR commissions work, what it pays is a term of that engagement, agreed in writing. Audience, credit and exposure are not a substitute for it, and no one is asked to produce work ZEVR commercially benefits from on the understanding that the reach is the compensation.

Terms for any specific piece of work — including whether and how it is paid — are agreed in writing before that work starts. ZEVR is not advertising compensation arrangements on this page, because its contracting structure is not finalised and publishing terms it cannot yet stand behind would be worse than saying nothing.

Creative and production

The people who make ZEVR's own work good.

Separate from the above, and just as deliberate: the craft roles behind what ZEVR itself publishes. Creative work here is contribution, credited and recorded with the same seriousness as competitive work.

Editors and motion

Coverage, highlights, and the pieces that make a season legible after it has happened.

Design

Everything the organization looks like in the world — inside a frozen identity, which is a constraint and the interesting part of the job.

Broadcast, writing and analysis

Calling matches, writing them up, and the analysis that makes a result mean something to someone who did not watch it.

Openings

None posted.

This is where creator opportunities and craft roles will both appear.

Nothing is open right now.

When ZEVR needs an editor, a designer or a broadcaster, the opportunity is posted here with what it is, who it reports to, and how it is decided. When creator programs open, the same.

There is no general portfolio inbox in the meantime. Inviting submissions with nothing to submit them to would waste the work of exactly the people this page is written for.
