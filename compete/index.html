Compete

Programs, not a logo on a jersey.

A ZEVR program is a roster, a coach, a schedule and someone accountable for it. The organization is built to run several at once, across different titles, without rebuilding itself each time.

ZEVR runs no competitive programs today. The first is being stood up in NBA 2K.

How it works

Designed multi-title from the first line of code.

Nothing in ZEVR's systems is hard-coded to one game, because an organization that is architecturally a 2K org cannot add a second title without a rewrite. That is design intent — no titles are running yet.

A program, defined

A title, a roster, a coach or manager, a schedule, and a named person answerable for it. A program does not start because someone wants a team; it starts because those five things exist.

Why programs are slow

Standing one up badly costs the players their season. ZEVR would rather run one program properly than announce four and staff none of them.

Titles

NBA 2K is first. Others follow when there are people to run them properly — not when there is an audience for the announcement.

Trials

What actually happens when you put your name in.

When a program opens trials, the path is the same for everyone, and no stage of it is skipped for someone who is already known.

You enter the open pool

No invitation, no referral, no follower threshold. When entry is open, it costs nothing and does not expire the moment a roster fills.

You are evaluated by people

Against the needs of a specific program, by someone who has to record why. Evidence from The Grind informs that judgement — it does not make it.

You get an answer

Including when the answer is no. A decision with a reason is the minimum; silence is the norm in this industry and it is not acceptable here.

A trial is not a commitment to sign you. It is a commitment to look properly and tell you what was decided. ZEVR does not promise roster places, playing time, or a career, and any page that did would be lying to the people it most wants to reach.
