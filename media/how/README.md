## The one condition: every Z is the canonical FORGED Z

The generated art carried non-canonical Z marks on jerseys, banners and
screens. For each crop: every generated Z was painted out (OpenCV inpaint over
the glyph's own bright pixels), and where a mark stands (the main stage
screens, the hero jerseys, the editor's jacket) the canonical symbol
`public/brand/ZEVR-SYMBOL-MASTER-REVERSE.svg`, rendered white at scale with its
geometry untouched, was composited back with a glow drawn from its own
silhouette. Banners and background jerseys are left plain. Do not replace these
with art carrying a non-canonical mark.

Sizes: each crop is about 2× its largest rendered size in the section, so it
stays sharp on a 2× display at 1920 px.
