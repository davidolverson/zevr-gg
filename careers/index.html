Careers

Nothing is open. Here is what will be.

ZEVR will need operators, coaches, analysts, producers and engineers. None of those roles are posted, and this page will not pretend otherwise while its structure is still being built.

Zero roles are open. There is no waitlist, no talent pool for staff roles, and no general application inbox.

Where things actually stand

The organization is not yet able to hire.

This is a straight answer rather than a soft one, because the alternative wastes the time of the people most likely to read this page carefully.

Structure first

Corporate structure, contracting and how people are engaged are being established. Until those exist, ZEVR cannot responsibly offer anyone a role.

No roles posted

Not one, and no count is implied anywhere on this site. When a role opens it will appear here, described properly.

No speculative inbox

There is nowhere to send a CV, deliberately. Collecting applications against roles that do not exist means people wait for an answer that was never possible.

Nothing on this page states or implies compensation, benefits, employment or contractor status. Those follow from a legal and contracting structure that is not finalised, and advertising them first would commit the organization to terms it cannot yet stand behind.

The work

What running this actually takes.

Not a roles list. A description of the functions a gaming-native company needs in order to operate across talent, media, technology, competition and culture.

Operations

The people who make a season happen: scheduling, logistics, and everything that is invisible when it works.

Coaching

Preparation, review and player development. Accountable for a program rather than attached to it.

Analysis

Turning what happened into something a coach and a roster can act on before the next match.

Production

Broadcast, streams and the technical craft of making competition watchable.

Community

The day-to-day of the people around the organization, and the standards that keep it somewhere worth being.

Engineering and design

The organization's own systems — identity, membership, competition and safety — and the surfaces people meet them through.

How hiring will work

The same standard as everything else here.

When roles do open, they run through ZEVR's own governed process rather than beside it.

Posted with the details

What the role is, who it answers to, and how the decision is made — before anyone spends an evening on an application.

Decided by people, with a reason

The same commitment that governs competitive selection. No automated system decides who is hired.

An answer either way

Including no. Silence after an application is the industry norm and it is not one ZEVR is adopting.

Leadership is not advertised here. Roles that run parts of the organization are reached through contribution inside it, not applied for from outside. An organization that recruits its leadership by advert gets people who wanted the title.

Openings

Zero.

Stated as a number so there is nothing to read between the lines of.

No roles are open at ZEVR.

When that changes, each opening appears on this page individually, with its own description and its own process. There will be no announcement that ZEVR is “hiring” without a specific role attached to it.

ZEVR is not currently able to engage anyone under 18 in any capacity, and is not inviting applications from minors.

ZEVR is a gaming-native company connecting talent, media, technology, competition, culture and partnerships.
