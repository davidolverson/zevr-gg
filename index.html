From signal to prospect

  1. Signal

    Something is changing: a title, a format, a platform.

  2. Evidence

    Where it was seen, and when. No source, no signal.

  3. Opportunity

    A person decides the change is worth pursuing.

  4. Prospect

    People worth a conversation, found through the work.

Scout everything. Pursue selectively.

Creator workflow

The stages every piece moves through

  1. Idea

    Intent and format agreed before anything is cut.

    • Draft

  2. Production

    Source captured and rights recorded. Unknown never counts as cleared.

    • Ready for review

  3. Edit

    Cut, reviewed, and signed off by the creator whose work it is.

    • Editing
    • Creator review

  4. Publish

    Designed so a person publishes, and only once rights are cleared.

    • Scheduled
    • Published

  5. Analyze

    Performance informs the next piece. It never becomes a score on the creator.

Program structure

What a program is built on

  • Events

    Formats, schedules, and someone accountable for each one.

  • Rosters

    Who is on a team, and since when, kept on the record.

  • Scrims

    Practice against real opposition, run to match standard.

  • Results

    Designed to be recorded once, not quietly rewritten.

  • Compliance

    Eligibility and integrity, meant to sit apart from event staff.

  • Community

    Discord is where people gather. It is not the record.

Partnership workflow

How a partnership would run

  1. Outreach

    A first conversation, in either direction.

  2. Discovery

    What the brand needs, and whether the fit is real.

  3. Proposal

    A written scope. Nothing is agreed until it is written down.

  4. Execution

    Content, campaigns and activations delivered as scoped.

  5. Measurement

    What happened, reported plainly. Expected value is not a result.
